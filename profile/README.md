  <h1>Koreji</h1>

  <p><strong>Turn your spare moments into meaningful progress.</strong></p>
</div>

Koreji helps you make the most of the small pockets of time throughout your day. Whether you're commuting, waiting, or between tasks, turn otherwise unused moments into meaningful progress on your work, learning, or personal projects.

More than just a timer, Koreji helps you see how small, consistent efforts add up over time—transforming scattered moments into tangible achievements.
